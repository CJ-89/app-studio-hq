# Closed test plan (12 testers × 14 continuous days)

## Recruit 15–20, not 12
People drop out; the requirement is about testers who stay opted in. Sources:
1. Friends, family, colleagues (Google suggests this)
2. Your communities, socials, the app's target user groups (they also give real feedback)
3. Developer tester-swap communities (reciprocal testing)
4. Paid tester services exist — use carefully; low-quality testers give no useful feedback

## Setup
- Create a Google Group (or email list) → add it to the closed track → share the opt-in link + Play listing link.
- Testers must opt in AND install from Play. Ask each to confirm with a screenshot.
- Keep the track running the whole 14 days — don't pause/halt it.
- Ship real updates during the test (shows active testing; useful for the production access questions).

## Daily rhythm
- Day 1: welcome message with what to test (3 tasks).
- Day 4 and day 9: short feedback form (3 questions).
- Fix S1/S2 bugs and push builds during the window.

## Production access application
You'll be asked about how you recruited testers, how engaged they were, what feedback you got and what you changed. Keep notes during the test (in LAUNCH_PLAN.md) so answers are specific. Thin answers are a common reason for rejection.
