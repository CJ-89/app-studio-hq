# App Store Optimization (Google Play)

## Listing fields
| Field | Limit | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| App name | 30 chars | Brand + main keyword, e.g. "Expensio: Budget Tracker" |
| Short description | 80 chars | Benefit + keyword, no hype words |
| Full description | 4000 chars | Indexed by Play search. First 2 lines = value; then benefits, features, social proof, premium explanation |

## Keyword research
1. List 20 phrases users would type (from planner research + competitor titles/short descriptions).
2. Check Play search autocomplete for each.
3. Pick 1 primary (in the name), 2–3 secondary (short description), the rest woven naturally into the full description (no keyword stuffing; Play penalises it).

## Avoid
"Best", "#1", "Free" as a claim in the title, emojis/caps-lock gimmicks in the title, competitor brand names, misleading screenshots.

## Visuals
Screenshot story and icon rules: see app-designer `store-assets.md`. Screenshots + icon drive conversion more than text.

## Experiments
After launch, use **Store listing experiments** in Play Console (icon, screenshots, short description) — one change at a time, run until significant.

## Localization
Translate the listing (and screenshot headlines) for each priority market; Play also offers machine-translated listings.

## Launch traffic (Play gives new apps little organic traffic at first)
- Communities where the target users are (Reddit, Discord, Facebook groups, niche forums in the US/UK/IN), short demo videos (TikTok/Reels/Shorts — your video automation skills apply), Product Hunt for tool-type apps, a simple landing page with the Play badge.
- Ask happy users for reviews via the in-app review API.
