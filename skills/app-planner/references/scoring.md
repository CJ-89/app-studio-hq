# Idea scorecard

Score each 1–5. Weighted total out of 100.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 = | 5 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand | 20 | No search/trend signal | Rising demand, many active apps |
| Gap / differentiation | 20 | No clear complaint to fix | Repeated complaints you can solve |
| Monetization | 15 | Nobody pays in this niche | Competitors earn with paid models |
| Build complexity (inverse) | 15 | Needs real-time, video, heavy backend | Mostly local, few screens |
| Distribution | 15 | No way to reach users except ads | You have an audience, SEO/ASO keywords, or community |
| Personal fit | 10 | No interest or skills | Strong skills, interest, edge |
| Policy risk (inverse) | 5 | Heavily regulated (health, finance, kids) | Low-risk category |

Total = Σ(score × weight) ÷ 5.

## Verdict
- **75+ GO** — build it.
- **55–74 PIVOT** — keep the problem, change the audience, angle or model. Suggest the specific pivot.
- **<55 NO-GO** — say so plainly and offer the next best idea.

Always show the table, not just the number.
